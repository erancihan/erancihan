Strengths
- Opens by naming the constraint honestly: the class cannot compile until init
  is finished, and says why (Swift requires every `let` assigned in init). The
  reader is never left wondering if they broke something.
- BlendMode is taught one case at a time with what each mode is *for* — additive
  explained as "what light actually does", alpha flagged as the only one where
  draw order matters. The reader ends knowing the look, not the API name.
- Real traps called out at the point of use: stride-not-size, the
  depthStencilPixelFormat omission that silently disables depth states, shader
  name typos producing an unhelpful "Pipeline creation failed", and the weak
  MTKView.delegate that makes `_ = preview` load-bearing.
- drawMesh split at the genuine seam — buffer binding (the instancing insight)
  separate from the indexed/non-indexed branch (bookkeeping).
- Checkpoint has five distinct symptom→cause entries, and asks the reader to
  break the lit pipeline's blend mode deliberately and observe.
- Canonical was reordered (BlendMode moved above Renderer) so the teaching order
  is also a valid dependency order rather than a forward reference.

Concerns (accepted)
- Longest chapter in the guide. Splitting 06 into A/B already halved it; a
  further split would separate init from the passes that depend on it.
- The per-draw instance buffer allocation is called out as a known cost with the
  fix named, rather than silently shipped as good practice.
