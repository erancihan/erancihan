# Review — chapter 07.A (Shaders & SPIR-V)

**Persona:** graphics engineer who has debugged a swapped vertex attribute.

**Verified:** 38.6% code, 13 blocks, largest 14 added lines, 100% anchored, lint
clean. **All eight shaders reconstruct exactly** and the reconstructed tree compiles
all eight to SPIR-V through the project's own CMake rule.

**What's right**
- The chapter's spine is the three numbering contracts, each named where it is
  created: vertex `location` ↔ pipeline attribute descriptions (checked by nobody),
  descriptor `set`/`binding` ↔ `VkDescriptorSetLayoutBinding` (checked at pipeline
  creation), and vertex `out` ↔ fragment `in` (checked by the GLSL compiler). Saying
  explicitly *which* of the three has no safety net is the useful part.
- The deliberate breakage is the strongest in the guide: swapping the two `location`
  numbers **compiles perfectly**, because both are `vec3`, and the damage does not
  appear until chapter 08. It demonstrates the "checked by nobody" claim rather
  than asserting it.
- Real explanations for lines that look decorative: `normalize(vNormal)` because
  interpolating two unit vectors shortens them; reversed `smoothstep` arguments;
  `discard` disabling early depth test; `gl_PointSize` being undefined-if-unwritten
  and needing `largePoints`.
- `unlit.vert` deliberately omitting `inNormal` is explained *forward* to 07.B's
  position-only vertex layout, so the reader knows it is intentional.
- The duplicated `InstanceData` declaration is called out as the project's most
  fragile seam rather than glossed over.
- `hud.frag` being a copy of `unlit.frag` is justified rather than deduplicated.

**Findings**
1. No defects found by the checker on this chapter — the first one to pass clean on
   the first attempt.
2. `-inPosition.y` in `hud.vert` is introduced here but only *used* in chapter 13.
   The forward reference is explicit, and putting the flip anywhere else would split
   the shader across two chapters, which is worse.

**Score: 9.5/10**
