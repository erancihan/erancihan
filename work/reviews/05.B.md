# Review — chapter 05.B (Commands, synchronization & the frame)

**Persona:** Vulkan practitioner who has personally shipped the first-frame deadlock.

**Verified — this is the strongest evidence in the guide so far.** The project was
reconstructed *purely from the chapter snippets* (`verify.py --emit`) and then:
- `cmake` configured and built it with **no errors or warnings**;
- it **ran** on lavapipe under validation layers;
- it printed the two expected lines and **nothing else** — validation silent;
- it survived an `xdotool` resize to 800×600 and back;
- the centre pixel measured `srgb(39,39,69)`, which is exactly the linear clear
  colour `(0.02, 0.02, 0.06)` after the sRGB encode the swapchain format performs.

So the chapter's promised checkpoint is observed, not asserted. 44.5% code, 25
blocks, largest 16 added lines, 100% anchored, lint clean.

**What's right**
- **Two derivations, both on real invariants.** (1) The unsignalled fence: the
  reader writes it, the program *hangs with no diagnostic at all*, and the prose
  names the circular wait before revealing `VK_FENCE_CREATE_SIGNALED_BIT`. (2) The
  `MAX_FRAMES_IN_FLIGHT = 1` experiment, which renders *identically* and costs half
  the throughput — with the trap called out explicitly: vsync hides it until your
  scene gets heavy.
- A third, quieter one that is arguably the most valuable: the resize edit **moves**
  `vkResetFences` below the early return, and the prose explains that this is the
  point of the edit — resetting before a possible bail-out reintroduces the same
  deadlock, but only on resize.
- `waitStage = COLOR_ATTACHMENT_OUTPUT` gets a real explanation (vertex work
  proceeds during the wait) rather than being copied through.
- The checkpoint asks for three specific stress actions (slow resize, fast resize,
  minimise/restore) and gives a 7-row symptom table including the two that produce
  *no* message.
- Deliberate breakage is two-part: magenta to prove you own the pixels, then a
  depth-clear change that provably does nothing *yet* — pre-loading the exact bug
  the reader will hit in chapter 08.

**Findings**
1. *(fixed)* Three diffs had no context lines — two were temporary experiments
   better expressed as prose instructions, one needed an anchor. Caught by the
   checker.
2. *(fixed)* The chapter claimed "3 images"; the measured run reported 4. Both
   chapters now say the count varies and that it means nothing about correctness.

**Score: 9.5/10**
